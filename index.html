About Me

Welcome. I offer empathetic peer guidance and active listening for individuals looking for a judgment-free space to talk through life's everyday challenges, personal choices, and academic pressures.

Please note: I offer non-clinical peer support and life guidance to help you talk things through. I am not a licensed medical professional, clinical psychologist, or psychiatrist.
